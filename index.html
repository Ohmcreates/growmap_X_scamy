How Scam Spotter Works

Scam Spotter runs a deterministic client-side heuristics engine across 16 scam categories. Your messages never leave your browser, ensuring 100% data privacy.

1. Exact Evidence Detection

Every urgency phrase, bank threat, and fake OTP verb is identified with zero hallucination.

2. Group Capping

Prevents runaway scores by capping correlated signals to avoid unfair false alarms.

3. Floor Rules

Verified phishing URLs and known malware domains lock the minimum score to High (60+) immediately.
